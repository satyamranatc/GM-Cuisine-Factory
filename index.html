About Us

Serving taste & tradition since day one. At GM Service Catering (GM Cuisine Factory), we craft gastronomic spectacles where ordinary food has no place in extraordinary moments.

We are Central India's premier luxury pure vegetarian catering atelier, designing bespoke banquets for royal weddings, silver jubilee anniversaries, high-profile corporate galas, and strict Jain religious ceremonies across Indore and Madhya Pradesh.

Our Sacred Culinary Commitments:
• 100% Pure Vegetarian & Dedicated Jain Cookware: Absolute sanctity, separate utensils, zero cross-contamination, pure cow ghee preparation, and strict onion-garlic-free choices upon request.
• Theatrical Live Culinary Stations: Uniformed executive chefs orchestrating sizzling tawa delicacies, artisanal Indori Sarafa chaat, and molecular liquid nitrogen Smoke Paan.
• Royal Presentation: Traditional hammered copper and brass chafing banquet displays with white-glove professional hospitality.
